Maps

One map and one timeline: the whole world with the seasonal snowfall painted on it, drawn from Natural Earth 1:50m data. The field is the model's own output - every grid cell the model came on, nothing resampled - and it can be read as the accumulated snowfall or as the anomaly against that model's own hindcast climate. The map can be the flat world on an equirectangular grid or the globe you can spin. Zoom in and out with the buttons on the map, with the scroll wheel or with the keyboard, and drag the flat map to pan it.
