The map's run total showed an empty field with a diagonal line

Three bugs, all in the exporter, all found by looking at the ECMWF's
anomaly on the live map.

THE RUN TOTAL'S ANOMALY WAS A NaN EVERYWHERE.  The run total is the sum
of the monthly anomalies, and a month with no hindcast climate
contributes NaN - and np.sum() of a NaN is NaN.  So the whole run total
came out masked, and the map's "Total" button showed an empty field with
a diagonal line across it.  Three models were affected: the UKMO, the
Météo-France and the ECCC canSIPS, plus the ECMWF once its extension
made it seven months.

The sum now skips the months that have no climate, so the run total is
the anomaly over the months it can be measured on.

THE RUN TOTAL'S MASK WAS NEVER APPLIED.  The monthly anomaly rasters
are masked where the model's own climate expects no snow, but the run
total's was written without the mask, so it showed an anomaly where
there is no snow at all.  It is masked now, like the monthly ones.

THE MULTI-MODEL'S MEAN CLIMATE WAS DILUTED.  The mean is taken over
the members that HAVE a climate.  A member without one contributes
zeros, and zeros say "the model expects no snow here", so including
them washed the mean climate down and nothing was masked at all - the
opposite of the right answer.

THE MULTI-MODEL ASSUMED EVERY MEMBER REACHES AS FAR.  The ECMWF
carries on past the sixth lead month with its extension, so it has
seven months where the others have six, and the mean ran off the end of
the other members' lists.  The mean is now taken over the months EVERY
member has.

And the manifest's per-lead "ym" was the LAST lead month's key in
every entry, because the loop used the outer loop's variable.  The
multi-model's month labels came out as null.  Both are fixed.
